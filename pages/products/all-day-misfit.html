Common Questions — All Day

Is All Day suitable for office use?

Yes. All Day is designed as a clean, elegant daily-wear fragrance that works well for office environments, meetings, and everyday routines without being overpowering.

Does All Day smell like coffee?

The opening has a dark coffee and cacao note, but it quickly develops into a warmer, woody gourmand on the skin. It is inspired by coffee rather than smelling like a coffee shop.

Is All Day a strong perfume?

All Day is a concentrated, long-lasting perfume. It is built for wearability — present and confident, but comfortable for all-day everyday use.

Is All Day good for everyday wear?

Yes. All Day is NAFUME's most versatile everyday fragrance — comfortable for office, college, travel, and casual settings. Its coffee and woody profile works well for both daily and professional wear.
