Common Questions — Dark Oud

Is Dark Oud suitable for daily use?

Dark Oud is an intense, bold fragrance best suited for evenings, festive occasions, and formal plans. Those who love deep woody scents can wear it anytime, but it may feel intense for light everyday use.

Is Dark Oud a good gift?

Yes. Its bold, premium oud profile makes it a strong gifting choice for birthdays, festivals, and anyone who loves deep woody fragrances.

What does Dark Oud smell like?

Dark Oud opens with spiced cardamom, deepens into aged oud and dark leather, and settles into a warm base of amber, frankincense and vetiver. Rich, layered, and long-lasting.

Is Dark Oud good for Indian weather?

Dark Oud performs best in the evening or in cooler, air-conditioned environments. In very hot or humid weather, apply sparingly — it projects strongly on warm skin.
