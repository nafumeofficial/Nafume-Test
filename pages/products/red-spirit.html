Common Questions — Red Spirit

What kind of fragrance is Red Spirit?

Red Spirit is a bold, spicy aromatic long-lasting perfume. It opens with cinnamon, ginger and clove bud, builds into cedarwood and vetiver, and finishes with tonka bean, dark musk and vanilla. Built for confident evenings and strong first impressions.

Is Red Spirit for men or women?

Red Spirit is a bold, unisex-leaning fragrance that works best for anyone who enjoys intense, spicy scents. Its spiced aromatic profile has broad appeal.

Is Red Spirit too strong for everyday use?

Red Spirit has a bold, projecting opening. For everyday use apply lightly. It performs best for evening outings, parties, and occasions where a strong presence is welcome.

Is Red Spirit good for gifting?

Yes. Its bold, confident profile makes it a strong gifting choice for festive seasons, birthdays, and anyone who prefers statement fragrances.
