How to Choose a Perfume as a Gift

By NAFUME  ·  Fragrance Guide  ·  June 2026

Perfume is one of the most personal gifts you can give — and also one of the trickier ones to choose without knowing exactly what the person likes. Fragrance is subjective, and a scent that's beautiful to one person can be overpowering to another. But with a bit of thought about the recipient's style and the occasion, you can make a confident choice.

In India, fragrance has always been part of the gifting tradition — from attar at weddings to perfume sets at Diwali and birthdays. NAFUME's premium packaging and versatile range makes it a gifting option that works across occasions without requiring you to know the recipient's exact fragrance preferences.

How to Read the Recipient's Style

Before choosing a fragrance, think about the person you're buying for:

  • What do they already wear? If you've ever noticed them wearing fragrance and liked it, that tells you something about their direction — fresh vs. warm, light vs. intense, floral vs. woody.
  • What's their personal style? People who dress minimally and prefer clean, understated aesthetics tend to prefer fresher, subtler fragrances. Those with bolder, more expressive style often appreciate something with more intensity and character.
  • What's their daily life like? An office-going professional will use a different fragrance daily than someone who works from home or is frequently in outdoor settings. Think about when they'll actually wear it.
  • How old are they? This isn't a hard rule, but younger buyers often start with fresh or light fragrances; more confident fragrance wearers often move toward depth, wood, and intensity over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is perfume a good gift in India?

Yes — fragrance is a personal and thoughtful gift with a long cultural history in India through attar, incense, and traditional perfumery. A well-chosen perfume shows care and attention. The main challenge is choosing something that suits the recipient, which is why this guide focuses on safe, broadly-appreciated choices.

What perfume should I gift if I don't know their preferences?

When uncertain, choose something clean, fresh, or gently floral rather than heavy or intense. Aqua Manthan is NAFUME's most broadly appreciated option — fresh and easy to wear across seasons and settings. Seduction works well as a warm or romantic gift for someone who appreciates something more personal.

What is a good perfume gift for a woman?

For women, Seduction is NAFUME's most popular gifting choice — warm, floral-oriental, and romantic. Aqua Manthan works for those who prefer something lighter and fresher. If she already wears oud or heavier fragrances, Dark Oud is a thoughtful choice.

What is a good perfume gift for a man?

Aqua Manthan is NAFUME's most universally liked men's gifting fragrance — fresh, clean, and confident. All Day suits men with a more individual style. For a bolder choice, Red Spirit or Dark Oud work well if you know the recipient likes intense fragrances.
