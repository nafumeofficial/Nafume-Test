Gifting FAQs

Which NAFUME perfume is best for gifting?

All five NAFUME perfumes come in premium packaging ready to gift. Seduction is the top pick for her — a romantic floral fragrance. Dark Oud is most popular for him — deep, bold and distinctively luxurious. For unisex gifting, Aqua Manthan is a safe and universally appreciated choice.

Does NAFUME offer gift packaging?

Yes. Every NAFUME fragrance arrives in elegant presentation packaging ready to gift. You can also add a personalised gifting message when placing your order on WhatsApp.

Can NAFUME ship a gift directly to the recipient?

Yes. Provide the recipient's address when confirming on WhatsApp and NAFUME will ship directly to them. Pan-India delivery in 3–7 business days.

What is the price for gifting — is ₹799 the final price?

Yes. Each fragrance is ₹799 for a 50ml bottle (original MRP ₹1,299). Shipping is free on orders above ₹999. For multiple gifts, the Build Your Own Box option lets you bundle up to three fragrances.

Are NAFUME perfumes good for corporate gifting?

Yes. Premium packaging, authentic long-lasting quality, and pricing that works for volume gifting. Contact the NAFUME team on WhatsApp to discuss bulk or corporate orders.
