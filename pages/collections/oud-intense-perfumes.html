Oud Perfume FAQs

Which NAFUME perfume smells most like oud?

Dark Oud is NAFUME's purest oud fragrance — aged agarwood, dark leather and saffron form the core, with frankincense and vetiver anchoring a deep, resonant base. It is the most authentic oud experience in the collection.

Is Dark Oud too intense for daily wear?

Dark Oud is bold and projects confidently. For most people, 1–2 sprays is enough for daily wear. It is best suited for evenings, formal events and celebrations. For daily office use, Aqua Manthan or Red Spirit would be more appropriate.

What is an oud perfume?

Oud (also called agarwood) is a resinous wood formed inside infected Aquilaria trees. It is one of the most prized fragrance ingredients in the world — known for its deep, smoky, woody and slightly sweet character. Oud-based fragrances are beloved across India, the Middle East and internationally.

How many sprays of Dark Oud should I use?

1–2 sprays on pulse points is enough for Dark Oud — it is a highly concentrated, long-lasting fragrance that projects strongly. Less is more. Apply to the chest for an intimate trail, or the wrist for more noticeable projection.

Yes. Dark Oud is NAFUME's most popular gifting choice for those who appreciate bold, premium fragrances. Both come in premium packaging ready to gift and can be shipped directly to the recipient across India. See the Gifting page for details.
