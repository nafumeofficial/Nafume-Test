Fresh Perfume FAQs

Which NAFUME perfume is the most fresh?

Aqua Manthan is NAFUME's freshest fragrance — an ocean-inspired perfume with sea salt, bergamot and mandarin orange opening, a marine accord and green tea heart, and a clean cedarwood and ambergris base. It is the definitive fresh pick in the collection.

Is Aqua Manthan good for summer in India?

Yes. Aqua Manthan is particularly well-suited for Indian summers — the marine accord and citrus top notes cut through humidity and heat naturally. The fragrance actually blooms more in warm weather, making it stronger in Indian summer than in cool climates.

Can I wear a fresh perfume to the office?

Yes. Fresh fragrances like Aqua Manthan are excellent office choices because they stay clean and pleasant without overwhelming colleagues in shared spaces. 2–3 sprays on pulse points is ideal for all-day office wear.

How long does Aqua Manthan last?

Aqua Manthan is a highly concentrated, long-lasting fragrance that typically projects for 6–8 hours on warm skin. The marine and cedarwood base keeps it going even after the fresh top notes fade. Applying to pulse points right after a shower gives the best longevity.
