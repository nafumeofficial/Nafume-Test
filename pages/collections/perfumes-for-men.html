Questions About Men's Perfumes

Which NAFUME perfume is best for men?

It depends on the occasion. Dark Oud and Red Spirit are bold and intense — ideal for evenings and formal moments. Aqua Manthan is the go-to for daily and office wear. All Day suits those who prefer something warm and unconventional through the day.

Are NAFUME perfumes strong enough for men?

Yes. All NAFUME perfumes are highly concentrated formulas built to last. Apply 2–3 sprays to pulse points — chest, wrists and neck — and the fragrance holds through long days in Indian weather.

Which NAFUME perfume lasts the longest for men?

Dark Oud and Red Spirit tend to project the most — both are warm, resinous fragrances that amplify on the skin's heat. Aqua Manthan is lighter but stays fresh for 6–8 hours with good application.

Are NAFUME perfumes suitable as gifts for men?

Yes. All NAFUME perfumes come in premium packaging suitable for gifting. Dark Oud is the top pick for men's gifting — intense, distinctive and unmistakably luxurious. See the Gifting page for more.
