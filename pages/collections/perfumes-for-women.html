Questions About Women's Perfumes

Which NAFUME perfume is best for women?

Seduction is the most popular — a warm romantic floral with Bulgarian rose, jasmine and amber, ideal for evenings and special occasions. Aqua Manthan is the top everyday choice — fresh, clean and versatile for office and daily wear. Red Spirit suits those who prefer bold, spicy warmth.

What is the best NAFUME perfume for a date night?

Seduction is made for date nights — its warm floral oriental profile with rose, jasmine and amber creates a lasting, magnetic impression. See the Date Night collection for more options.

Which NAFUME perfume is best for everyday office wear for women?

Aqua Manthan is the best everyday and office choice — fresh sea salt, bergamot and green tea notes stay clean and pleasant without overwhelming a shared space.

How long do NAFUME perfumes last on women?

All NAFUME fragrances use a high parfum concentration designed for long-lasting wear. On warm skin, Seduction and Red Spirit typically last 6–10 hours. Aqua Manthan is lighter and freshens for 6–8 hours. Apply to pulse points after a shower for best results.

Can I buy NAFUME perfumes as a gift for women?

Yes. All NAFUME perfumes come in premium packaging ready to gift. Seduction is the most popular gifting choice for women. Visit the Gifting page for all gift-ready options and personalisation.
