Unisex Perfume FAQs

What is a unisex perfume?

A unisex perfume is a fragrance built to be equally appealing and wearable regardless of gender. They tend to focus on versatile, universally appreciated scents — fresh ocean notes, clean woody bases, warm spices, gourmand warmth — rather than strongly gendered profiles.

Which NAFUME perfumes are unisex?

Aqua Manthan, All Day and Red Spirit are NAFUME's most worn unisex fragrances. Dark Oud crosses gender preferences naturally — oud is a traditionally shared scent in Indian and Middle Eastern culture.

Which unisex NAFUME perfume is best for everyday wear?

Aqua Manthan is the best everyday unisex choice — a fresh ocean-inspired fragrance with sea salt, bergamot and green tea that works in any setting. All Day is the second best option for those who prefer a warm gourmand character.

Are unisex perfumes different from regular perfumes?

No. Unisex simply indicates the scent works well across genders. The quality, concentration and longevity of NAFUME's unisex perfumes are the same as any other fragrance in the collection.

Can I gift a unisex NAFUME perfume?

Yes. Unisex fragrances make great gifts because they're universally appreciated. Aqua Manthan and All Day are both popular gifting choices. All NAFUME perfumes come in premium packaging ready to gift.
