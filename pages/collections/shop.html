Shopping FAQs

How do I choose a perfume online?

Start with the kind of scent you enjoy — fresh, woody, floral, spicy or sweet — and where you'll wear it most. Use the filters above to narrow by occasion and scent family, or try the fragrance finder for a guided pick.

Which perfume is best for daily or office wear?

Fresh, clean scents like Aqua Manthan work well for daily and office wear because they stay close and pleasant. See the Office & Daily Wear collection above.

Are NAFUME perfumes long-lasting?

All NAFUME fragrances use a high parfum concentration, designed for a smooth, long-lasting wear. Applying to pulse points after a shower helps them last longer.
