Office & Everyday Perfumes

A great office perfume stays close to the skin, stays fresh through a long workday, and never overwhelms a shared space. NAFUME's everyday picks — Aqua Manthan, Red Spirit and All Day — each fit that brief in their own way. Aqua Manthan is the obvious daily driver: sea salt, bergamot and green tea open fresh and clean, settling into warm cedarwood and clean musk. Red Spirit layers cinnamon and spice over a warm cedarwood heart — confident enough to be noticed, refined enough for a boardroom. All Day takes dark coffee, tobacco and vanilla into something surprisingly wearable and warm through the whole day. All three are highly concentrated, long-lasting perfumes at ₹799 — built to hold through Indian office hours.

Choosing an Everyday Perfume for Indian Wear

An everyday perfume for Indian conditions needs to hold through heat, humidity and long commutes without becoming overwhelming. Aqua Manthan achieves this naturally — the marine accord and citrus top notes cut through heat while the cedarwood base keeps the fragrance grounded. Red Spirit's warm spice-woody base is excellent for air-conditioned offices and cool mornings. All Day is the choice for those who want their fragrance to tell a story — the coffee and tobacco accord is unusual, wearable and consistently conversation-starting. All three are highly concentrated, long-lasting perfumes at ₹799. Apply 2–3 sprays to pulse points for best all-day performance.

Office & Everyday Perfume FAQs

Which NAFUME perfume is best for office wear?

Aqua Manthan is the top office pick — its fresh sea salt, bergamot and green tea opening stays clean and professional in shared spaces. Red Spirit works for those who want something warm and confident at work. All Day's coffee and vanilla warmth is great for casual or creative office environments.

Is Aqua Manthan a good daily perfume?

Yes. Aqua Manthan is NAFUME's most versatile daily wear perfume — fresh, clean and long-lasting. It works in the office, outdoors, at the gym and through casual social settings. The marine accord, bergamot and green tea combination is universally appreciated and non-intrusive.

How many sprays should I use for office wear?

2–3 sprays on pulse points (chest, inner wrists, neck base) is ideal for an office setting. NAFUME fragrances are highly concentrated, so a light application goes a long way. For shared office spaces, 1–2 sprays is enough.

Are NAFUME perfumes too strong for office?

With the right application, no. Aqua Manthan and All Day wear close to the skin and are not overpowering. Red Spirit is bolder — use 1–2 sprays for office settings. The key is applying to pulse points rather than spraying into the air.
