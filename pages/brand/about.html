About NAFUME

NAFUME is a premium Indian perfume house creating long-lasting, highly concentrated fragrances for real life — the heat, the humidity, the long workdays and the evenings that follow. Refined, wearable, and made to last the whole day.

Our Story

A fragrance is an invisible signature — it speaks before you do and lingers after you leave. NAFUME was created to give that signature to everyday wear, with blends that feel premium without being overwhelming.

Rather than chase loud, mass-market scents, we focus on smooth, memorable compositions built around carefully chosen notes — and tuned for how fragrance actually behaves in Indian conditions. The result is perfume that stays present and balanced from morning to night.

Why NAFUME

Built for Indian Weather

Formulated to perform in heat and humidity — engineered for our climate, not for European winters.

Highly Concentrated

A high perfume-oil concentration means fewer sprays and longer wear — true value at ₹799.

Clean Ingredients

Phthalate-free, triclosan-free, and never tested on animals. Gentle on skin, considered in every detail.

Our Fragrances

Each NAFUME perfume is a highly concentrated, long-lasting blend designed to hold through Indian weather. From fresh and aquatic to deep oud and warm spice — there is a scent for every mood and moment.
